Support

Last updated · 2026-10-23

Retouch edits photos and Live Photos on your iPhone. Find help with Pro memberships, restoring purchases and managing local data here.

Monthly, yearly and Lifetime Pro

All three plans provide the same Pro access: the full look library, advanced photo enhancement, and Live Photo enhancement, cleanup and cover selection. Free tools remain available, and cancelling does not delete saved drafts. Lifetime Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase with no renewal; its price is shown in the in-app Apple product and confirmation screen. Buying or redeeming Lifetime Pro does not automatically cancel an existing monthly or yearly subscription. Cancel renewal in system subscription settings if you no longer need it.

Restore purchases

  1. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase or redemption under Media & Purchases on your iPhone.
  2. Open Retouch Pro from the profile area on the home screen, or Retouch Pro / Restore purchases in the app's settings.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases and complete any Apple verification.
  4. After Apple verifies an active subscription or an unrevoked Lifetime Pro transaction, the page displays the corresponding access.

Restoring does not purchase a second membership, and you do not need to cancel membership first. If no valid purchase is found, check the Apple Account used for the purchase or redemption, subscription expiry or Lifetime Pro transaction status. If Apple synchronization fails, check the network and account sign-in, then retry or contact support; you do not need to purchase again. Restoration does not recover drafts, photos or private-album items.

Redeem an official Apple offer code

If you have an eligible official offer code, tap Redeem Apple offer code on the membership page and complete Apple's redemption confirmation. The offer and Apple's screen specify the product, eligibility and redemption deadline. Closing the sheet does not confirm redemption; the app updates access from transactions verified by Apple. If access has not appeared after redemption, use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account.

Cancel renewal or request a refund

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions to manage Retouch. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Apple handles purchases and refunds through Report a Problem.

Photos and drafts

The app browses only your authorized library on your device. Tap a photo to preview it, swipe between photos, and play Live Photos when LIVE is enabled. Tap the top-right checkmark to import the current photo for editing. Original photos, face-analysis data and editing results are not uploaded to the developer or model providers. Editing takes place on the device and saving creates a new copy. Drafts and the private album have no developer cloud backup. Export results before uninstalling or switching devices.
