Privacy Policy

Last updated · 2026-10-23

Photo editing and face analysis take place on your device. Retouch edits photos and Live Photos locally, without sending photos or face-analysis data to the developer or model providers. Apple handles membership purchases and restoration.

1. Scope and operator

This policy covers the iOS app Retouch (修好颜), provided by PEIWEI YANG (杨培伟), and this privacy and support website. Contact us at 1059919879@qq.com for privacy or support questions.

The app does not require an account with the developer and includes no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics. We do not sell personal information or use your photos or facial data to train models.

2. Photos, Live Photos and face data

2.1 Face data processed on your device

The app reads the images you select and, for imported Live Photos, the paired video and audio. Those images and video may themselves contain faces. Source files and local copies retained for continued editing can contain metadata such as capture dates. The app does not request device-location permission.

On your device, the app detects face regions and positions, facial contours, and landmarks for eyes/irises, eyebrows, nose and lips. It also calculates pose, detection confidence and local masks needed for editing. Automatic beauty temporarily analyses skin brightness, texture, color casts and facial proportions to derive edit settings. For Live Photos, it tracks face position and movement within that video. In this policy, face data includes these analysis results and the person-position anchors retained in drafts as described below.

2.2 Purposes and limitations

Face data is used only for the local editing you request: positioning beauty, skin, makeup, eye-refinement, face-reshaping effects; selecting a person in a multi-person photo; locating portrait-enhancement regions; keeping Live Photo effects aligned with movement; and protecting an unselected main subject's head during Live Photo object removal. The app does not identify real people, recognize people across photos, create biometric identity templates, or use photos or face data for advertising, profiling, sale or model training. Body reshaping separately uses on-device body-pose detection and regions you adjust. There are no other planned uses.

2.3 Uploading and third-party access

Face analysis runs on your device. The app does not automatically send your photos, face landmarks, masks, position anchors or editing results to the developer. These are not automatically uploaded to the developer, Google, model authors or third-party AI services. There is no remote face database or developer-operated cloud draft service. User-directed export/sharing and system iCloud services are described in Section 5; they do not send facial analysis to an AI service.

Photos permissions

  • The in-app browser reads only the authorized photo list and thumbnails on your device, supports full-size preview, swiping and Live Photo playback, and creates an editing draft only after you confirm the current photo. It retrieves that asset's original image or paired Live Photo resources by its identifier.
  • You can limit or revoke permission in system settings. Unauthorized photos do not appear in the in-app browser. The system-picker fallback supplies only the items you select and imports them as still photos.
  • Saving to Photos requests permission to add photos and creates a new copy without replacing the original. Denying access may prevent the requested import or save.
  • Apple's Photos service may download selected resources from iCloud. Subsequent iCloud syncing follows your system settings.

3. Face-data storage, retention and deletion

3.1 Temporary facial analysis

Full face landmarks/meshes, pose, detection results and masks are working data for the current editing or processing task, held in in-memory processing caches; they are not saved as full facial-analysis records in draft files. Leaving the editor clears its facial-analysis and rendering caches. Live Photo tasks release their working data when completed, cancelled or replaced. Reopening a photo runs analysis again. After abnormal termination, the system reclaims process memory; image-related temporary disk files follow Section 3.4.

3.2 Data actually retained in drafts

Ordinary drafts do not have the private album's separate encryption and authentication. They are stored in the app sandbox's local draft directory. They contain source image/Live Photo resource copies, working images, previews, editing settings, undo/redo history and accepted enhancement results. These images may contain faces. Multi-person editing also saves rectangular person-position anchors within that photo, local editing identifiers and each person's edit settings in the draft and relevant history, to reapply edits at the corresponding positions when reopening that photo. These are not real-world identity identifiers or cross-photo face templates. Live drafts may also retain paired video, audio and source asset identifiers.

Drafts and their position anchors remain until you delete the draft or delete the app; there is no fixed automatic expiry. Deleting a draft in the app removes its folder, including source copies, working files, previews, edit/history records and person-position anchors. Draft directories are excluded from system backups, and there is no developer cloud sync. Revoking Photos permission does not delete previously imported drafts. Deleting the app removes its local files; iOS Offload App retains documents and data and is not a substitute for deleting drafts. Restore Purchases cannot recover deleted drafts or photos.

3.3 Private album and system authentication

When you choose to move a result into the private album, the app requests system Face ID, Touch ID or device-passcode authentication, saves and verifies an AES-GCM encrypted result, then removes the ordinary draft and its edit history. A subsequent cleanup failure is reported. The private album retains encrypted finished photos, optional Live Photo videos and item information, not editable facial meshes or the original draft history. Still previews are generated in memory as needed; Live Photo previews may create the temporary decrypted copies described in Section 3.4. Items remain until you delete them in the private album or delete the app, with no fixed automatic expiry.

The encryption key stays in this device's Keychain and is not cloud-synced. The app receives only the system authentication outcome, not Face ID/Touch ID biometric templates, and does not store those templates. The album locks when closed or when the app enters the background. Private contents are excluded from backups, with no recovery password or transfer service; deleting the app, removing the device passcode or changing devices can make contents unrecoverable.

3.4 Temporary files and exported copies

Processing, Live Photo playback and sharing may create temporary files containing face images. Decrypted private-result files are normally cleaned up after the relevant operation; interruption can delay this until the next private-store initialization. Ordinary Live Photo processing and sharing clean up applicable files on normal completion; stale folders left by interruption are checked at cleanup opportunities such as the next app launch and removed when older than 24 hours. This is not a guarantee of immediate cleanup on exit or automatic deletion within 24 hours.

Deleting a draft or private item does not delete the original in Photos or previously exported copies; manage these separately in Photos, Files or the receiving app. The developer cannot remotely access, delete or recover content stored only on your device.

4. Pro memberships and payments

Pro includes monthly, yearly and Lifetime Pro plans with the same benefits. Monthly and yearly plans are Apple App Store auto-renewable subscriptions; Lifetime Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase with no automatic renewal. Eligible official offer codes are handled through Apple's redemption interface. StoreKit supplies product information, prices, verified transaction and product identifiers, subscription expiry dates and revocation status to grant, update and restore access. Purchases, redemption and restoration connect to Apple's services and may require Apple Account authentication.

Apple handles payment and related account information. The app receives neither payment-card details nor your Apple Account password, and sends no transaction records to a developer server. Photos, facial analysis and editing results are not part of purchase verification. Apple's processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

You can cancel monthly or yearly renewal in Apple's subscription settings. Access continues during the valid paid period as verified by Apple. Deleting the app, purchasing Lifetime Pro or redeeming a Lifetime Pro offer does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Restore Purchases uses the Apple Account associated with the purchase or redemption to verify active subscriptions and unrevoked Lifetime Pro access. You do not need to cancel membership first. Restoration does not recover local editing data.

5. On-device AI, export and third-party services

5.1 Local models and third-party AI

The app does not call third-party online AI services, generative-AI APIs or remote model-inference services, and sends no photos, video, audio, face data or other user data to such services. It uses third-party open-source technology bundled with the app: MediaPipe Face Landmarker for on-device face positioning; Real-ESRGAN-based Core ML models for local image enhancement; and LaMa- and MI-GAN-based Core ML models for local object removal/inpainting. It also uses on-device Apple Vision, Core ML, Core Image and Metal. Models run on your device without delivering inputs to model authors. Usage/performance telemetry transmission has been removed from the bundled MediaPipe implementation. The app includes no third-party analytics SDK and does not train models on user photos.

5.2 User-directed system and external services

Only when you choose to save, export or share does the app hand a result to your selected photo library, file destination or receiving app through system interfaces. Recipients receive the media you choose to export, not the app's internal face landmarks, masks or person-position anchors. Receiving services and any cloud sync follow their own policies. Exported private results are no longer protected by the private album's lock.

Apple Photos may download iCloud resources you preview or import; iCloud sync after saving to Photos depends on your system settings. StoreKit connects to Apple for product prices, purchases, redemption and restoration, without sending photos or face data. Opening public privacy/support webpages or voluntarily emailing support uses the relevant website or email service, as described in Section 6. These connections are not used for cloud AI editing.

Local settings, file timestamps, storage availability and processing durations support settings, file management, low-storage notices and processing. They are not used for advertising or device fingerprinting.

6. Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer questions, resolve issues and retain necessary support records. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment-card details or unrelated private photos. We do not automatically retrieve photos or diagnostic files from your device.

Support records are kept only as needed to handle and follow up on the request or meet necessary legal obligations. You may email us to request access, correction or deletion. Email providers process delivery. This website has no developer-added analytics, advertising or tracking tools; its hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs for delivery and security under its service policies.

We cannot remotely access, delete or recover photos, drafts or private contents stored only on your device. We respond according to your request and applicable requirements, verifying only information needed to handle it. Support correspondence is generally processed to provide assistance at your request; security and required retention may rely on legitimate interests or legal obligations.
